# 复古手绘明亮配色修订

用户认可保留复古线条与造型、配色改为明快轻松的方向。使用内置 imagegen 编辑原图，非 CLI。

- 输入：[较暗的复古候选](style-retro-2026-09-21.png)
- 输出：[明亮配色候选](style-retro-bright-2026-09-22.png)
- 目视检查：角色、主要对象与构图保留；泛黄和深暗背景明显减少，蓝色天空/水面、绿色植物与红黄音乐对象更鲜明。具体调色稿仍待用户反馈；不是已验收游戏资产。

## 完整提示词

```text
Edit this original hand-drawn music adventure concept image. Change ONLY the color treatment and brightness, preserving the composition, all objects, character designs, expressions, poses and bold hand-inked outlines.
Keep the original cheerful walking metronome character, the friendly bell, three chimes, drum, garden and gate exactly where they are. Preserve the traditional cartoon linework, elastic limbs and hand-painted animation look.
Make the scene distinctly brighter, lighter and more welcoming. Remove the overall brown/sepia cast, dirty paper stains, heavy old-film grain and muddy dark shadows. Use a clean light warm-cream base, a fresh pale blue sky, brighter spring green foliage and clear turquoise blue water. Use cheerful golden yellow for bell and chimes, a brighter warm coral/red drum rim, and clean warm wood on the metronome, with light cream face and gloves. Retain crisp dark outlines for readability. Lift large dark background masses to lighter green and blue-green, reduce shadow density, keep color contrasts legible. Flat cel-painted foreground colors with gentle watercolor background, sunny daytime mood. Keep subtle handmade texture but no aged parchment patina. Do not simply raise exposure or wash out the blacks: recolor thoughtfully while retaining confident ink lines. No 3D rendering, no new objects, no text, no book, no UI. The result should feel like fresh, colorful hand-drawn animation rather than a dark antique print.
```
