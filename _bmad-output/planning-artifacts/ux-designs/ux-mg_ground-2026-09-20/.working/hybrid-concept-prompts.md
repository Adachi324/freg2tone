## 初版提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one polished original game art concept comparison board for a music-learning adventure aimed at adult beginners. One wide image with TWO equally sized landscape gameplay frames stacked vertically, a discreet small arrow between them. They show the SAME scene in flat illustrated form (top) and dimensional 3D diorama form (bottom). No written labels, no explanatory paragraphs, no logo. This is art direction exploration, not final UI.
User preferences: expressive hand-drawn contour lines, inviting storybook illustration atmosphere, tactile page-turn feeling, and the delight of entering a 3D world from a 2D plane. Critically WEAKEN THE BOOK METAPHOR: absolutely no visible book, binding, spine, cover, school desk, classroom, textbook, flashcards or worksheet. The first impression is playing a charming game.
Scene: an original tiny music-mechanism garden with a small simple explorer character, three tactile sounding pieces integrated into a compact mechanical path, and a little passage that these pieces can open. Music objects are recognizable as sound-making things, not a piano keyboard or multiple-choice buttons. Same explorer, same mechanism, same objects and same color identities in both frames. Use restrained hand-painted color accents and confident slightly irregular ink outlines, subtle paper grain, generous readable negative space. No copied characters or specific scenes from existing games.
TOP: genuinely flat 2D illustrated composition, front-facing or map-like graphic layout with no volumetric rendering. All main pieces immediately readable, room along one edge for future short contextual explanation, but no actual text or teaching panels. This plane is itself playable, not a lesson page.
BOTTOM: the exact same illustrated layout has become a small tangible 3D world, viewed with a fixed three-quarter camera. Objects now have physical depth, low platforms, soft shadows and foreground/background relationships, while KEEPING the ink outlines and hand-painted surfaces of the top. Avoid generic shiny plastic toys or photorealism. A gently curling strip of the scene's own illustrated ground at one corner suggests it has just unfolded or peeled upward like a page, without any book. This transition is a proposed visual metaphor, not the only possible design.
Maintain matched compositions so the 2D-to-3D continuity is unmistakable. High-quality playful atmospheric concept art, legible mobile-scale forms, not childish classroom graphics. No musical theory claims or note-name correspondences.
```
